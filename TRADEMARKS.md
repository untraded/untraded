# Untraded trademark policy

Untraded's code is free software (AGPL-3.0-only, and MIT for the libraries in `packages/`).
**The Untraded name and logo are not**: they identify the project and the service run by its
maintainers, so that people know what they are getting. This policy says how you may use them.
It is inspired by the policies of Rust, Mozilla and Matrix.

The marks covered: the name "Untraded", the "cote" logo (two squares linked by a diagonal) and the
wordmark, in every version in this repository (`docs/brand/`, the site's icons and its `Logo`
component). The Untraded Authors hold the rights to them. They are not covered by the code's
licences: their files are marked `LicenseRef-Untraded-Trademark` (see `REUSE.toml`).

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to refer to the project or the service: in an article, a video, a talk, a
  comparison, a list of tools, a link.
- Use the logo, unchanged, to link to the project or the service, or to show that your tool works
  with it ("works with Untraded").
- Run your own instance of Untraded **unmodified**, for yourself, your team or your club, and call
  it Untraded.
- Say that your project is based on Untraded or forked from it ("based on Untraded").

## Ask first

- Selling merchandise with the name or logo.
- Using the name or logo in an event or a sponsorship.
- Running an instance of Untraded, modified or not, as a **service for others** under the
  Untraded name.

Write to nicolasgirardot60@gmail.com. We usually say yes to community uses.

## You may not

- Use the name or logo for a **modified version** (a fork), a hosted service of your own, or a
  product that is not Untraded. Pick another name and logo, and say it is "based on Untraded".
- Modify the logo: change its colours, shapes or proportions, combine it with another mark, or add
  effects to it.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests a partnership, a sponsorship or an endorsement that
  does not exist.
- Use a name, a logo or a domain name that could be confused with Untraded's (for example
  "Untraded Pro", "untraded-stats.gg").

## Questions
